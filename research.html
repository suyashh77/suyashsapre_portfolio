Active Research Work

Climate Resilience Lead-Time Framework

In Development · Dr. Rachunok · NC State

Active

Quantitative framework modeling how long a state has to prepare as its hazard profile shifts due to climate change. As North Carolina's risk profile transitions toward hurricanes, flooding, drought, and wildfire, the question becomes: how much lead time do communities and infrastructure systems actually have to adapt? Combines historical hazard data, climate projections, and infrastructure vulnerability modeling.

Python Geospatial Analysis Climate Data Infrastructure Modeling

Food Desert POI Data Collection

Ongoing · Rachunok Lab

Active

Built a scalable Google Maps scraper collecting point-of-interest data for grocery stores and food retailers across North Carolina — approximately 12,000 locations and 12 million reviews. The dataset supports research on food access disparities, infrastructure resilience, and community vulnerability. Tools: Python, Selenium, PostgreSQL.

Interests

What I Want to Study

My research identity is at the intersection of machine learning and classical operations research. I'm interested in when ML can make OR faster, more robust, or applicable to problems that were previously too large or uncertain to solve well.

Reinforcement Learning for Vehicle Routing

Attention-based models (Kool et al.) and RL policies that learn good heuristics for VRP variants. When does learned heuristic beat exact methods?

Stochastic Optimization Under Demand Uncertainty

Two-stage stochastic programs, robust optimization, and scenario generation for supply chain decisions under demand variability.

Predict-then-Optimize Frameworks

End-to-end differentiable optimization layers; integrating ML prediction with OR prescription in a single pipeline.

Infrastructure Resilience & Climate OR

Modeling infrastructure system failures, recovery optimization, and preparedness lead times under evolving climate hazard profiles.
